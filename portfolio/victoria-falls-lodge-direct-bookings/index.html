Direct bookings for a lodge near Victoria Falls

How we would help a small lodge near Victoria Falls win more direct bookings, with a booking-ready website, clear answers for AI search, proper tracking and ads aimed at travellers abroad and Zimbabweans visiting home.

This is an example project: an illustration of how we would plan and deliver work for a business like this. It is not a past client engagement, and the business described is not a real client.

The brief

What the business needs

In this example, the business is a family-run lodge with a handful of chalets on the edge of Victoria Falls town, close to the Zambezi National Park. Guests come for the falls, river cruises and game drives, and for a quieter stay away from the town centre. Most bookings arrive through online travel agencies and tour operators, so the lodge pays commission on almost every night it sells. The owners want more guests to book direct on the lodge's own website, and they want to know which marketing brings those bookings in.

The challenge

International travellers plan months ahead, compare many options on booking sites and read reviews closely, so a small lodge has to look trustworthy at every step. Many know little about travelling in Zimbabwe and want clear answers on transfers, payments and what to expect. The booking engine runs on a separate system, so tracking often breaks between the website and the booking, and nobody can see which channel earned the sale. Travellers are also starting to ask AI assistants where to stay, and those answers lean on places whose details are clear and consistent across the web.

Our approach

How we would tackle it

Fix the tracking first

We would set up GA4 and Google Tag Manager with cross-domain tracking to the booking engine, so every direct booking is recorded with its value and source. That would give an honest starting point for comparing direct bookings with those from agencies.

Make the website the easiest place to book

We would build a fast, mobile-first site with clear room pages, rates and inclusions, a getting-here guide and the booking engine one tap away on every page. Any perks for booking direct, such as flexible dates or airport transfers, would be stated plainly. Honest photos and video would be shot on a visit arranged with the owners.

Become a clear answer for AI and search

We would write question-led pages on getting to the falls, transfers, activities and how the falls change between high and low water. We would add lodging schema and match the lodge's details across Google Business Profile, Tripadvisor and booking sites. Each month we would check how ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity answer a fixed set of travel questions.

Advertise to people already planning a trip

Google search ads would cover Victoria Falls accommodation searches and the lodge's own name, so booking sites do not catch people already looking for it. Meta ads would reach travellers in the UK, the US and South Africa, and Zimbabweans abroad planning a family holiday back home. Retargeting would remind visitors who checked dates but did not book.

Deliverables

What we would deliver

  • Mobile-first website with room, activity and getting-here pages
  • Booking engine integration with cross-domain tracking and booking values in GA4
  • Data Studio dashboard comparing direct and agency bookings
  • Question-led content, lodging schema and consistent listings across the web
  • Google and Meta campaigns for international, regional and diaspora travellers
  • Monthly AI answer checks across ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity

How we would measure success

  • Direct bookings and booking value, compared with agency bookings
  • Website booking conversion rate by country
  • Cost per direct booking from paid campaigns
  • How often the lodge is named in AI answers to travel questions
  • Visits from organic search and AI assistants
