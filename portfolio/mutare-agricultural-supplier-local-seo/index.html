Local SEO and farming content for an agricultural supplier in Mutare

How we would help a Mutare farm supplies business get found by farmers across Manicaland, through a stronger Google Business Profile, local search pages and practical seasonal guides in English and Shona.

This is an example project: an illustration of how we would plan and deliver work for a business like this. It is not a past client engagement, and the business described is not a real client.

The brief

What the business needs

In this example, the business is an established agricultural supplier in Mutare. It sells seed, fertiliser, crop chemicals, irrigation fittings, stockfeed and small equipment, and has an agronomist on hand to advise customers. Its customers range from smallholders around Mutare to commercial growers further afield in Manicaland. It has a basic website and a Google Business Profile that nobody has updated in years. The owner wants more farmers to find the shop when they search, to call or WhatsApp before travelling into town, and to see the business as a trusted source of advice.

The challenge

Buying is seasonal. Demand peaks before the summer rains, when farmers stock up on seed and fertiliser, so much of the year's trade can fall into a few busy months. Farming in Manicaland is also very varied, from bananas in Burma Valley and potatoes in Nyanga to tea, coffee and macadamias around Chipinge, so one generic message will not fit everyone. Many farmers search on a phone with limited data, often in Shona. A trip into Mutare costs time and money, so they want to check prices and stock before they set off. Larger national chains compete for the same searches too.

Our approach

How we would tackle it

Fix the Google Business Profile

We would claim and complete the profile with the right categories, opening hours including seasonal changes, products and fresh photos of the shop and stock, taken on a visit arranged with the owner. Weekly posts would announce new arrivals, and a simple review request at the till and on WhatsApp would help build genuine reviews from farmers.

Build pages farmers actually search for

We would create product category pages for seed, fertiliser, crop chemicals, irrigation and stockfeed, plus pages for the farming areas the business serves, such as Odzi, Penhalonga, Burma Valley and Rusape. Each page would load quickly on mobile data and make calling or messaging on WhatsApp easy.

Publish practical seasonal guides

A content calendar would follow the farming year: land preparation, planting, top dressing, pest alerts such as fall armyworm, and winter crops. Short guides written with the shop's agronomist, in English and Shona, would be easy to share on WhatsApp and Facebook, and would give Google and AI tools clear, local answers to quote.

Keep listings consistent and measure what matters

We would correct the business name, address, phone number and hours across Zimbabwean directories and social profiles, and look for links from farming associations and local media. Calls, direction requests and WhatsApp chats would be tracked, so the owner can see each month what search brings in.

Deliverables

What we would deliver

  • Claimed and fully completed Google Business Profile, with products and weekly posts
  • Review request cards for the till and a WhatsApp review message
  • Product category and service-area pages for the farming areas served
  • Seasonal content calendar with guides in English and Shona
  • Listing clean-up across Zimbabwean directories and social profiles
  • Monthly local search report covering calls, directions and WhatsApp chats

How we would measure success

  • Calls, direction requests and website clicks from the Google Business Profile
  • Map visibility for key product searches in Mutare and nearby areas
  • Organic visits to product pages and seasonal guides
  • WhatsApp enquiries started from the website and profile
  • Genuine Google reviews gained each month
