Enrolment campaign for a private school in Bulawayo

How we would help an independent school in Bulawayo fill places for the January intake, with Meta ads timed around open days, focused landing pages and quick, friendly follow-up on WhatsApp.

This is an example project: an illustration of how we would plan and deliver work for a business like this. It is not a past client engagement, and the business described is not a real client.

The brief

What the business needs

In this example, the business is an independent co-educational day school in one of Bulawayo's low-density suburbs, running from ECD through primary to A Level, with Cambridge and ZIMSEC pathways. Most new families find it through word of mouth. Enquiries arrive by phone, Facebook message and WhatsApp, and a busy admissions office answers them when it can. The head wants more enquiries from the right families before the January intake, fuller open days and a clear view of where each new pupil came from.

The challenge

Choosing a school is a big decision, and fees are a long-term commitment, so parents compare several schools and take their time. Many Bulawayo families have a parent working in South Africa, so the person paying the fees may not be in Bulawayo at all. Enrolment also runs to a fixed calendar. Ads that start late miss the families who decided weeks earlier. And when a WhatsApp enquiry waits days for a reply, the parent has often moved on to the next school.

Our approach

How we would tackle it

Learn how parents choose

We would start with video calls with the head and the admissions team, and list the questions parents ask most, from fees and class sizes to transport and sport. Then we would map the enrolment calendar: open days, assessment dates and application deadlines for each entry point.

Build a landing page for each entry point

We would create separate, fast-loading pages for ECD, Grade 1, Form 1 and A Level entry. Each would answer parents' real questions and include a short video tour, an open day booking form and a WhatsApp button. Fees would be shown the way the school prefers, on the page or sent on request.

Run Meta ads around the calendar

Facebook and Instagram campaigns would reach parents in and around Bulawayo, using photos and short videos filmed on a school visit arranged with the head. Pupils would appear only with their parents' consent. Ads would run in English, with Ndebele versions tested alongside. A smaller campaign would reach Bulawayo families living in South Africa, and retargeting would remind parents who viewed a page but did not book.

Follow up every enquiry on WhatsApp

We would set up WhatsApp Business with a greeting, quick replies and labels for each stage, plus a simple sequence: prospectus, open day reminder, application form, assessment date. Every enquiry would go into one tracker, so the admissions office can see who is waiting and nobody goes unanswered.

Deliverables

What we would deliver

  • A landing page for each entry point, with open day booking
  • Meta campaigns with video, carousel and lead ads, planned around the intake calendar
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads and a WhatsApp Business set-up with quick replies and labels
  • Follow-up messages for prospectus requests, open day reminders and application steps
  • Enquiry tracker for the admissions office, with a weekly summary
  • Tracking for open day bookings, WhatsApp chats and applications

How we would measure success

  • Enquiries by entry point and by channel
  • Cost per open day booking
  • Time to first reply on WhatsApp enquiries
  • Enquiries that go on to applications and assessments
  • Applications that become confirmed places
