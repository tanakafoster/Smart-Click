Our story

Digital marketing, done properly, for Zimbabwe

Smart Click Media is a digital marketing agency based in Harare. We help businesses across Zimbabwe, from start-ups to established national brands, get found online, get chosen over their competitors and grow their revenue.

We combine specialist skills in SEO, AI search, paid advertising, websites and social media with a deep understanding of the Zimbabwean market: how people search, how they shop on their phones, how much they rely on WhatsApp, and how to reach the millions of Zimbabweans living abroad.

We're not interested in vanity metrics. Every campaign we run is judged on one thing: whether it grows your business.

Our mission

To help Zimbabwean businesses compete and win online, with honest, measurable digital marketing that pays for itself.
