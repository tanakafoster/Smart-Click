Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about working with Smart Click Media and about digital marketing in Zimbabwe.

About Smart Click Media

Where is Smart Click Media based?

We're based in Harare, Zimbabwe, and we work with businesses in every province, from Bulawayo and Mutare to Victoria Falls, Gweru and Masvingo, as well as brands targeting Zimbabweans abroad.

What services does Smart Click Media offer?

Digital marketing strategy, website design and development, SEO, AEO (AI search optimisation), paid ads on every major platform (Google, YouTube, Microsoft, Meta, WhatsApp, TikTok, LinkedIn, programmatic, and ChatGPT where available), Google Analytics and tracking, social media management, local SEO, e-commerce, email and WhatsApp marketing, lead generation and CRM, conversion rate optimisation, content marketing, and website hosting and maintenance.

When can I contact you?

Any time. We're open 24/7. Call or WhatsApp 0771 269 979, or email info@smartclick.co.zw.

How do I get started?

Call or WhatsApp us on 0771 269 979, email info@smartclick.co.zw, or fill in the form on our contact page. We'll set up a free strategy session, look at your current marketing and suggest a plan.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with start-ups, small and medium businesses and large organisations. We'll be upfront about the budget needed to get results, and we won't sell you services you don't need.

How do you report on results?

You get a clear monthly report covering enquiries, sales, spend and the work done, plus a review call. We focus on business results rather than likes and impressions.

Can you market to Zimbabweans in the diaspora?

Yes. We regularly target Zimbabweans in South Africa, the UK, the US, Australia and elsewhere for property, education, groceries, hampers and other services.

Can you create content in Shona or Ndebele?

Yes. Campaigns and content can be produced in English, Shona or Ndebele, depending on what connects best with your audience.

About our services

What budget do I need for digital marketing in Zimbabwe?

It depends on your goals and industry. Many local SMEs start with a modest monthly ad budget plus our management fee, then scale once the numbers prove out. We'll recommend a budget in your free strategy session and explain exactly where every dollar goes.

We're not in Harare. Can you still help us?

Yes. Our team is in Harare, and we work with businesses in Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo, Victoria Falls and every other province. Most of the work happens online, on WhatsApp and on video calls.

How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes 3 to 6 weeks from brief to launch. Larger e-commerce or custom builds take longer, and you'll get a timeline before we start.

Will I own my website?

Yes. You own the domain, the content and the site itself. We'll make sure all logins are in your name.

How soon will SEO show results?

You'll usually see early movement in 2 to 3 months, with strong results building over 6 to 12 months. Anyone promising page one in a week is not being honest with you.

Do you guarantee number one rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking, because Google controls the results. What we do guarantee is transparent work, clear reporting and a strategy built on what actually moves rankings.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation. It means improving how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews describe and recommend your business.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO is about ranking in search results. AEO is about being named, and described correctly, in AI-generated answers. The two overlap a lot: AI tools lean on websites that are easy to crawl, well organised and widely trusted, so strong SEO makes AEO much easier. We usually run them together.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, Performance Max and more), YouTube, Microsoft Advertising, Meta (Facebook, Instagram and Messenger), click-to-WhatsApp and WhatsApp Status ads, LinkedIn and programmatic display. TikTok and ChatGPT ads are available for diaspora campaigns run by businesses registered in countries where those platforms sell ads. Pinterest, Snapchat, X, Reddit, Spotify, Apple and Amazon ads are available on request, where the platform accepts your business and target market. The main platforms each have their own page with the details.

What should our ad budget be?

That depends on your goals, your industry and the value of each new customer to your business. We usually start with a test budget, learn what works, then scale up. Your ad spend is paid directly to the platforms.

What is Google Analytics 4?

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is Google's free analytics tool. It shows how people find and use your website, and which channels lead to enquiries and sales. It replaced the older Universal Analytics in 2023.

Why don't my Google Ads and Facebook numbers match Google Analytics?

Each platform counts results with its own rules and time windows. We set up consistent tracking across all of them, plus one dashboard, so you can compare channels fairly.

Which platforms should my business be on?

It depends on your audience. Facebook and WhatsApp reach the widest Zimbabwean audience, TikTok and Instagram skew younger, and LinkedIn is key for B2B. We'll recommend where to focus rather than spreading you thin.

Do you create the content?

Yes. We plan, write, design and edit content, and can arrange photo and video shoots in Harare and other cities.

My business doesn't have a shop. Can I still rank locally?

Yes. Service-area businesses, like plumbers, caterers and consultants, can rank on Google Maps by setting a service area instead of showing an address.

How important are Google reviews?

Very. Reviews affect both your map ranking and whether people choose you. We'll help you build a steady flow of genuine reviews from real customers.

Which platform is best for an online store in Zimbabwe?

WooCommerce (on WordPress) gives the most flexibility for local payment options. Shopify is great for brands selling regionally or internationally. We'll recommend the right fit for you.

Can customers pay with mobile money?

Yes, we can integrate locally available payment gateways that support mobile money and cards, depending on your bank and provider.

Does email marketing work in Zimbabwe?

Yes, especially for B2B, professional services and e-commerce. Paired with WhatsApp, which most of your customers already use every day, it gives you a low-cost way to follow up every lead.

Will WhatsApp broadcasts get my number blocked?

Not if it's done properly. We only message people who've opted in, and we follow WhatsApp's business policies.

Which CRM do you recommend?

For most small and medium sales teams, Pipedrive is simple and effective. HubSpot suits businesses that want marketing and sales in one platform. We'll recommend the best fit for your budget and process.

Can you connect WhatsApp to a CRM?

Yes, using official integrations and the WhatsApp Business API we can log WhatsApp conversations against contacts in many CRMs.

What's a good conversion rate?

It varies by industry and offer, but many sites convert only 1 to 3 percent of visitors. Small improvements to that number can make a big difference to revenue.

Do I need lots of traffic for CRO?

A/B testing works best with steady traffic, but even smaller sites benefit hugely from UX audits and best-practice fixes.

Do you use AI to write content?

We use AI tools to speed up research, but every piece is planned, written and edited by people who know your industry. Thin, generic AI content doesn't rank or win trust.

How many articles do I need each month?

Quality beats volume. For most businesses, a few genuinely excellent pieces a month outperform many short, shallow posts.

Can you take over a website someone else built?

Yes. We'll audit it first, then take over hosting and maintenance, or recommend a rebuild if it's beyond saving.

Will moving hosting affect my email?

We'll plan any move carefully, including your domain and email settings, so nothing gets lost.
