FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about working with Smart Click Media and about digital marketing in Zimbabwe.

About Smart Click Media

Where is Smart Click Media based?

We're based in Harare, Zimbabwe, and we serve businesses in every province, from Bulawayo and Mutare to Victoria Falls, Gweru and Masvingo.

What services does Smart Click Media offer?

Digital marketing strategy, website design and development, SEO, AEO (AI search optimisation), paid ads on Google, Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn, social media management, local SEO, e-commerce, email and WhatsApp marketing, lead generation and CRM, conversion rate optimisation, analytics, content marketing, and website hosting and maintenance.

How do I get started?

Call or WhatsApp us on 0771 269 979, or fill in the form on our contact page. We'll book a free strategy session, review your current marketing and suggest a plan.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with start-ups, SMEs and large organisations. We'll be honest about what budget is needed to get results, and we won't sell you services you don't need.

Do you lock clients into long contracts?

No. We prefer to earn your business month by month. Your ad accounts, website and data always remain yours.

How do you report on results?

You get a clear monthly report covering leads, sales, spend and what we did, plus a call with the specialist who runs your account. We focus on business results, not vanity metrics.

Can you market to Zimbabweans in the diaspora?

Yes. We regularly target Zimbabweans in South Africa, the UK, the US, Australia and elsewhere for property, education, groceries, hampers and other services.

Do you speak Shona and Ndebele?

Yes. We can create campaigns and content in English, Shona and Ndebele, depending on what connects best with your audience.

About our services

How much should I spend on digital marketing in Zimbabwe?

It depends on your goals and industry. Many local SMEs start with a modest monthly ad budget plus our management fee, then scale once the numbers prove out. We'll recommend a budget in your free strategy session and explain exactly where every dollar goes.

Do you work with businesses outside Harare?

Yes. We're based in Harare but work with clients in Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo, Victoria Falls and every other province. Most of our work happens online, on WhatsApp and on video calls.

How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes 3 to 6 weeks from brief to launch. Larger e-commerce or custom builds take longer, and we'll give you a clear timeline up front.

Will I own my website?

Yes. You own the domain, the content and the site itself. We'll make sure all logins are in your name.

How long does SEO take to work?

You'll usually see early movement in 2 to 3 months, with strong results building over 6 to 12 months. Anyone promising page one in a week is not being honest with you.

Do you guarantee number one rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking, because Google controls the results. What we do guarantee is transparent work, clear reporting and a strategy built on what actually moves rankings.

What budget do I need for Google or Facebook ads in Zimbabwe?

You can start small and still learn a lot. We'll recommend a test budget based on your industry and goals, then scale what works. Your ad spend is paid directly to the platform, separate from our fee.

Can ads send people straight to WhatsApp?

Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp ads are one of the most effective formats in Zimbabwe, and we set them up with tracking so we can measure the chats they produce.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation. It's the work of making your business show up, and be recommended, in answers from AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI Overviews.

Is AEO the same as SEO?

No, but they're closely linked. SEO earns rankings on Google. AEO earns recommendations from AI. AEO relies on the same foundations as good SEO, a crawlable site, quality content and authority, so the two work best together.

Which platforms should my business be on?

It depends on your audience. Facebook and WhatsApp reach the widest Zimbabwean audience, TikTok and Instagram skew younger, and LinkedIn is key for B2B. We'll recommend where to focus rather than spreading you thin.

Do you create the content?

Yes. We plan, write, design and edit content, and can arrange photo and video shoots in Harare and other cities.

My business doesn't have a shop. Can I still rank locally?

Yes. Service-area businesses, like plumbers, caterers and consultants, can rank on Google Maps by setting a service area instead of showing an address.

How important are Google reviews?

Very. Reviews affect both your map ranking and whether people choose you. We'll help you build a steady flow of genuine reviews from real customers.

Which platform is best for an online store in Zimbabwe?

WooCommerce (on WordPress) gives the most flexibility for local payment options. Shopify is great for brands selling regionally or internationally. We'll recommend the right fit for you.

Can customers pay with mobile money?

Yes, we can integrate locally available payment gateways that support mobile money and cards, depending on your bank and provider.

Is email marketing still worth it in Zimbabwe?

Yes, especially for B2B, professional services and e-commerce. Combined with WhatsApp, which almost everyone uses, it's one of the cheapest ways to turn leads into sales.

Will WhatsApp broadcasts get my number blocked?

Not if it's done properly. We only message people who've opted in, and we follow WhatsApp's business policies.

Which CRM do you recommend?

For most small and medium sales teams, Pipedrive is simple and effective. HubSpot suits businesses that want marketing and sales in one platform. We'll recommend the best fit for your budget and process.

Can you connect WhatsApp to a CRM?

Yes, using official integrations and the WhatsApp Business API we can log WhatsApp conversations against contacts in many CRMs.

What's a good conversion rate?

It varies by industry and offer, but many sites convert only 1 to 3 percent of visitors. Small improvements to that number can make a big difference to revenue.

Do I need lots of traffic for CRO?

A/B testing works best with steady traffic, but even smaller sites benefit hugely from UX audits and best-practice fixes.

Why do my Google and Facebook numbers not match?

Each platform uses its own attribution rules. We set up consistent tracking and a single dashboard so you can see a fair picture across all channels.

Can you track WhatsApp clicks?

Yes. We track clicks on WhatsApp buttons and links as conversions, and can go further with CRM integrations.

Do you use AI to write content?

We use AI tools to speed up research, but every piece is planned, written and edited by people who know your industry. Thin, generic AI content doesn't rank or win trust.

How many articles do I need each month?

Quality beats volume. For most businesses, a few genuinely excellent pieces a month outperform many short, shallow posts.

Can you take over a website someone else built?

Yes. We'll audit it first, then take over hosting and maintenance, or recommend a rebuild if it's beyond saving.

Will moving hosting affect my email?

We'll plan any move carefully, including your domain and email settings, so nothing gets lost.
