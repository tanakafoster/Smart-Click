Click-to-WhatsApp ads: a practical guide for Zimbabwean businesses

How to run Click-to-WhatsApp ads in Zimbabwe: app or Platform, linking accounts, objectives, pre-filled messages, fast replies, tracking and costs.

7 November 2026 · 7 min read · By the Smart Click Media team

For many Zimbabwean customers, WhatsApp is where buying decisions happen. A Click-to-WhatsApp ad on Facebook or Instagram opens a chat with your business in one tap, so people can ask a question, get a price and order without visiting a website. This guide covers the set-up choices, the ad itself, your first replies, the handover to sales and how to measure what your money brings back.

What a Click-to-WhatsApp ad is

A Click-to-WhatsApp ad is a Facebook or Instagram ad with a button that opens a WhatsApp chat with your business. It can run in feeds, Stories and Marketplace. Instead of landing on a website, the customer lands in a chat with a message ready to send.

Businesses based in Zimbabwe can run these ads and aim them at people in Zimbabwe or in diaspora markets such as South Africa, the UK, the US and Australia. You pay your ad spend directly to Meta, which charges as the ad is shown, not per chat, so an ad few people act on costs more per conversation.

Ads in WhatsApp Status can open a chat too, but they're bought alongside Instagram Stories and aren't offered everywhere (not in the UK or the EU, for example), so check Ads Manager first.

Step 1: Choose the WhatsApp Business app or the Platform

Both work with Click-to-WhatsApp ads. The difference is how you handle the chats.

WhatsApp Business appWhatsApp Business Platform
SuitsSmall businesses with one number and a small teamLarger teams, several branches or high chat volumes
Set-upA free app on your phoneThrough a provider, or a direct connection built by developers
CostFree to useMeta charges per message, and providers may add their own fees
ToolsBusiness profile, greeting and away messages, quick replies, labels, catalogue, broadcastsMessage templates, list and reply buttons, automated flows with handover to staff, CRM connections

On the Platform, these pricing rules apply as of November 2026:

  • Template messages, such as offers and order updates, are charged even inside the customer service window: the 24 hours after the customer's last message. The exception is the free window described below.
  • Since 1 October 2026, Meta also charges for service messages (your ordinary replies). Rates vary by country code, with Zimbabwe's +263 numbers in Meta's "Rest of Africa" group. Check the current rate card, and any free allowance, before you budget.
  • When someone messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad using WhatsApp on their phone, and you reply within 24 hours, a free entry point window opens and can stay open for up to seven days. Meta doesn't charge for your replies or templates in it.

Start with the app while you test. Move to the Platform when a busy team must share one number or you want automation and a CRM link, and plan the move with your provider. Our email and WhatsApp marketing team sets up both.

Step 2: Connect WhatsApp to your Facebook Page and Instagram

  • Choose one business number that is always answered, and use it everywhere: ads, website, Google profile and receipts.
  • Connect it to the Facebook Page that will run your ads, in the Page's settings, and confirm that you control the number. If the menus have moved, Meta's Help Centre has the current steps.
  • Connect your Instagram professional account to the same Page or business portfolio, so the ads can also run on Instagram under your Instagram name.
  • Make sure the business owns the Page, ad account and number, and add staff as people and your agency as a partner in your business portfolio.

Step 3: Choose the right campaign objective

In Ads Manager, Click-to-WhatsApp ads can run under the Engagement, Leads, Sales or Traffic objective. Set the conversion location to messaging apps, choose WhatsApp, and match the objective to your goal:

Your goalObjectiveOptimise for
Enquiries, quotes and bookingsEngagement or LeadsConversations
Product ordersSalesConversations at first, then purchases once you report sales back to Meta

Don't optimise for link clicks, reach or impressions: Meta will then look for people likely to tap or see the ad, not start a chat. Meta advises running an ad for at least seven days, so don't change it daily. Target only the towns you serve, and give each diaspora country its own campaign.

Step 4: Write the ad and the pre-filled message

A good ad tells people what you sell, roughly what it costs, which areas you serve and what happens when they tap, for example: "Tap for a quote on WhatsApp. We reply within 15 minutes, 7am to 9pm." Only promise what your team can keep, and use real photos or short videos.

The pre-filled message is the text waiting in the chat box, which the customer can edit before sending. Meta's default, "Hello! Can I get more info on this?", tells you nothing. Write your own, with a short reference for each ad so your team knows which one brought the chat:

  • "Hi, I'd like a quote for a home solar system in Harare. (Ref: SOLAR-HRE)"
  • "Hi, please send this week's hamper list for delivery to Bulawayo. (Ref: HAMPER-UK)"

Step 5: Set up your greeting and quick replies

In the WhatsApp Business app, set up:

  • A greeting message, sent automatically when a new customer messages you. Thank them, say when a person will reply, and ask the one question that helps most, such as their area.
  • An away message for outside your hours, with an honest reply time.
  • Quick replies for prices, delivery areas and fees, payment methods (including for buyers abroad), your location and hours, and how to order.
  • A catalogue with photos and prices, so people can browse while they wait.

On the Platform, your provider can add list and reply buttons and simple automated flows. Always offer a quick way to reach a person.

Step 6: Answer fast

Someone who has just tapped your ad is at their most interested. Keep them waiting and they may message a competitor instead.

  • Put someone in charge of the chats whenever ads are running, with a rota for evenings and weekends.
  • Diaspora buyers message in their own time: a buyer in the US may write while you're asleep, and evenings in Australia fall in your late morning or early afternoon.
  • On the Platform, reply within 24 hours to open the free entry point window. Once 24 hours pass after the customer's last message, you can only send approved templates.

Step 7: Hand over to sales

  • Agree who owns each chat and when it passes to a salesperson.
  • Collect the same details every time: name, area, what they need, budget, timing and how they will pay.
  • Label every chat, for example new lead, quote sent, paid and delivered.
  • Put quotes and invoices in writing.
  • Log each lead and its ad reference in one place: a CRM or, to start with, a simple spreadsheet.

Step 8: Measure conversations and sales

Ads Manager shows how many conversations your ads started and what each cost. Your own records show how many became sales. Each week, for each ad reference:

  1. Count the chats, quotes and sales.
  2. Divide ad spend by sales to get your cost per sale.
  3. Compare it with your profit per sale.
  4. Move budget to the ads with the lowest cost per sale, not the cheapest chats.

On the Platform, ask your provider whether it can send confirmed sales back to Meta through the Conversions API, so Ads Manager can report, and optimise for, purchases rather than just chats. For ads that go to your website first, track WhatsApp clicks as key events in Google Analytics 4 (see our GA4 checklist).

Common mistakes

  • Leaving Meta's default pre-filled message, so you can't tell your ads apart.
  • Judging success by cost per chat instead of cost per sale.
  • Vague ads with no price, area or offer, which bring chats from people you can't serve.
  • Using a staff member's personal number instead of one the business controls.
  • Adding everyone who messages to broadcasts without asking first.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the WhatsApp Business Platform to run Click-to-WhatsApp ads?

No. Click-to-WhatsApp ads work with the free WhatsApp Business app, which suits most small businesses starting out. The Platform makes sense when a busy team needs to share the same number, or when you want automation and a CRM connection. Meta charges per message on the Platform, but messages are free during the window of up to seven days that opens when you reply to an ad chat within 24 hours.

How much do Click-to-WhatsApp ads cost?

You choose the budget in Meta Ads Manager and pay your ad spend directly to Meta. Meta charges as your ad is shown, so your cost per chat depends on your targeting, your offer and how many people go on to message you. The WhatsApp Business app itself is free to use. On the Platform, Meta charges per message, based on the message type and the country of the number you message. Some messages are free, such as those sent in the window of up to seven days that opens when you reply to an ad chat within 24 hours.

Can Click-to-WhatsApp ads reach Zimbabweans living abroad?

Yes. Target the countries or cities where your buyers live, such as Johannesburg, London or Sydney, and make the ad clearly about Zimbabwe, for example groceries delivered to Harare. We don't target people by nationality. We use location, your own customer lists and ads that clearly speak to people buying for family at home. Run a separate campaign for each country, and reply during the buyer's waking hours.
