GA4 set-up checklist for Zimbabwean business websites

A practical GA4 set-up checklist for Zimbabwean businesses: currency, key events, WhatsApp and call tracking, consent, AI referrals and monthly reviews.

7 November 2026 · 7 min read · By the Smart Click Media team

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is free, but a quick install mostly tells you how many people visited and which pages they viewed. It won't show how many people tapped your WhatsApp or call buttons, sent an enquiry or bought something unless you set it up to. This checklist takes Zimbabwean business owners and marketing managers through a proper set-up, from creating the property to a monthly review. Use it on a new website or to audit the property you already have.

1. Create the property and web data stream

  • Create it under a Google account your business owns, then add your agency or developer as users.
  • In Admin, click Create, then Property. Pick your industry, business size and objectives, which decide the reports you see first.
  • Under Data collection and modification, open Data streams, click Add stream, choose Web and enter your domain.
  • Copy the Measurement ID (it starts with G-) for your tag.

2. Set the time zone and currency

Time zone: choose Zimbabwe, so report days match your working day. Changing it later affects only new data.

Currency: choose USD. GA4 converts only the currencies on its supported list, and when we checked in November 2026, ZWG (Zimbabwe Gold) wasn't on it. If you sell in ZWG, convert to USD in your website code before the value reaches GA4. If you send prices in rand, pounds or another supported currency, GA4 converts them to USD at the previous day's exchange rate.

3. Turn on enhanced measurement, and know its limits

In your web stream's settings, make sure Enhanced measurement is on. With no extra code it records page views, scrolls (only when a visitor reaches 90 per cent of the way down a page), outbound clicks, site searches, embedded YouTube plays (if the player's JavaScript API is on), file downloads such as PDF price lists, and form starts and submissions.

It won't give WhatsApp, call or email clicks their own named events, confirm a submitted form actually reached you, or record sales.

4. Install the tag once

  • Google tag: paste the snippet into the head of every page, or use your platform's built-in Google integration.
  • Google Tag Manager: add a tag for your G- ID that fires on every page. We prefer Tag Manager: the click tracking below is easier to build there.

Use one method, not both, or you risk counting visits twice. Check it with Tag Assistant and the Realtime report, allowing up to 30 minutes for new data.

5. Track WhatsApp, calls, emails and forms

In Tag Manager, give each enquiry action its own event:

  • WhatsApp: a Just Links click trigger where Click URL contains wa.me (add api.whatsapp.com if your buttons use it), firing a GA4 event named whatsapp_click.
  • Calls: Click URL starts with tel:, event phone_click.
  • Email: Click URL starts with mailto:, event email_click.
  • Forms: generate_lead, Google's recommended event for leads, sent only on a confirmed submission, such as the thank-you page loading.

No Tag Manager? GA4's Create event option can build whatsapp_click from outbound clicks whose link_url contains wa.me. Never send names, phone numbers or email addresses to GA4: Google requires you to keep personally identifiable information out.

6. Mark key events

GA4 calls your important actions key events. Google Ads calls them conversions. Mark an action as a key event in GA4, then, once Google Ads is linked, create a conversion from it so campaigns can optimise towards it.

  • When your events appear, open Admin, then Events under Data display, and click the star beside each one. Free properties allow up to 30 key events.
  • Mark business actions only: whatsapp_click, phone_click, email_click, generate_lead and, for shops, purchase.
  • Consider "once per session" counting for leads, so someone tapping WhatsApp three times counts once.
  • Key events count from when you mark them, not before.

7. Filter out your team and developers

  • In the web stream's tag settings, define internal traffic using your office's fixed IP address, then test and activate the Internal traffic filter under Data filters.
  • Create and activate a Developer traffic filter too, so test visits sent in debug mode stay out.
  • IP rules mainly catch staff on fixed office lines, because mobile data addresses change often.
  • Active filters are permanent: GA4 doesn't keep the data they exclude.

8. Extend data retention

In Admin, open Data retention. If event data retention says 2 months, change it to 14 months, the longest a free property allows. The setting affects explorations and funnel reports, not standard reports. Do it on day one: data that has already expired can't be brought back.

9. Cross-domain measurement and unwanted referrals

If visitors move between your own domains, such as your main site and a separate shop, add them all under Configure your domains in the tag settings, so GA4 sees one journey. If customers pay on a payment gateway's own domain and return to your thank-you page, add that domain under List unwanted referrals, so the gateway doesn't take credit for sales.

10. Link Google Ads and Search Console

  • Google Ads: in Admin, under Product links, link your Ads account, keep auto-tagging on, and create conversions from your key events. Our Google Ads management includes this as standard.
  • Search Console: link your verified site to see Google search queries and landing pages in GA4, then publish the Search Console reports from the Library.

11. Add e-commerce events for online stores

Send the standard events, including view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout and purchase. Each purchase needs a unique transaction_id, the value, the currency and the items. Shop plugins can send these, but place a real test order and check it arrives once, in USD, with the right amount. If many orders close on WhatsApp, track WhatsApp clicks from product pages too, so you can at least see which products start those conversations.

12. Consent and Consent Mode for UK and EU visitors

This matters whenever your site gets visitors from the UK or Europe, and especially if you advertise to Zimbabweans living there. Google's EU user consent policy covers visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland. You need their consent for personalised ads, and for measurement where the law requires it.

  • Consent Mode passes each visitor's choice to Google's tags. Version 2 added ad_user_data and ad_personalization alongside the original ad_storage and analytics_storage.
  • For EEA visitors, Google requires these signals, via Consent Mode or the IAB's consent framework (TCF), to keep its measurement, remarketing and personalised ads features working. It says it doesn't expect a verified signal for UK or Swiss traffic, but UK privacy law still applies.
  • Consent Mode isn't a banner. Pair it with a consent management platform that supports Consent Mode.
  • Basic mode keeps Google's tags off until the visitor agrees, and sends nothing if they decline. Advanced mode sends cookieless pings while consent is denied, so Google can model some of the gaps.
  • Since 15 June 2026, Consent Mode settings in Google Ads, not GA4's Google signals setting, control whether Google Ads cookies and IDs are collected through your GA4 tag.

13. See visits from AI assistants

GA4's default channel group now includes an AI Assistant channel for visits from tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Copilot and Grok. Find it in the Traffic acquisition report under Reports. It excludes Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, which GA4 counts as Organic Search.

If an assistant your buyers use, such as Perplexity or Claude, still lands in Referral, create a custom channel group under Admin, Data display, Channel groups: copy the default group, add a channel where Source matches the regex .*(perplexity|claude)\.ai.*, and place it above Referral so it matches first. Treat totals as a minimum: some visits arrive with no source. Getting named in those answers is the job of AEO.

14. Build a Data Studio dashboard

Data Studio (formerly Looker Studio) is Google's free dashboard tool, and it connects directly to GA4. Build one page your team will read: key events by channel and by type, top landing pages, AI Assistant visits and Search Console queries.

15. Monthly review routine

  1. On mobile data rather than office Wi-Fi, tap your own WhatsApp button and phone link, and check both appear in Realtime. Your internal traffic filter hides visits from the office.
  2. Compare key events by channel with last month and the same month last year.
  3. Find landing pages with plenty of visits but few key events, and fix those first. That is where conversion rate optimisation pays off.
  4. Review AI Assistant visits and Search Console queries.
  5. Compare GA4 key events with Google Ads conversions and look into big gaps.
  6. Annotate site changes, price changes and campaign launches.

Common mistakes

  • Sending ZWG prices labelled as USD, which inflates revenue.
  • Leaving data retention at 2 months.
  • Treating enhanced measurement's form_submit as confirmed leads.
  • Marking page views or scrolls as key events.
  • Letting an agency or freelancer own the account.
  • Remarketing to UK and EU visitors with no consent set-up.

Our Google Analytics and tracking service can audit your property against this list.

Frequently asked questions

Should my GA4 property use USD or ZWG?

Use USD. GA4 only converts currencies on its supported list, and when we checked in November 2026, ZWG was not on it. If you sell in ZWG, convert prices to USD in your website code before they are sent to GA4, so your revenue figures stay accurate.

What is the difference between a key event and a conversion?

A key event is an action you mark as important in GA4, such as a WhatsApp click or a completed enquiry form. A conversion is what Google Ads uses to measure and optimise your campaigns. Once GA4 and Google Ads are linked, you can create Google Ads conversions from your key events.

Can GA4 show visits from ChatGPT and other AI assistants?

Yes. GA4's default channel group now includes an AI Assistant channel for visits from tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot. It doesn't cover Google's AI Overviews or AI Mode, and some visits arrive without a source, so treat the figure as a minimum.
