FAQ

SEO questions, answered

How long does SEO take to work?

You'll usually see early movement in 2 to 3 months, with strong results building over 6 to 12 months. Anyone promising page one in a week is not being honest with you.

Do you guarantee number one rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking, because Google controls the results. What we do guarantee is transparent work, clear reporting and a strategy built on what actually moves rankings.

What's the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO gets you ranked on Google. AEO gets you recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Good AEO is built on top of good SEO, so we usually do both together.

Can you do SEO for a specific city like Bulawayo or Mutare?

Yes. Local SEO targets customers in specific towns and suburbs, and it's one of the best-value things a local business can invest in.
