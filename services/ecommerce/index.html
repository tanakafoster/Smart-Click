FAQ

E-commerce questions, answered

Which platform is best for an online store in Zimbabwe?

WooCommerce (on WordPress) gives the most flexibility for local payment options. Shopify is great for brands selling regionally or internationally. We'll recommend the right fit for you.

Can customers pay with mobile money?

Yes, we can integrate locally available payment gateways that support mobile money and cards, depending on your bank and provider.

Can you help me sell to Zimbabweans abroad?

Yes. Diaspora-focused stores, for groceries, hampers, building materials and gifts, are a big opportunity, and we know how to market them.
