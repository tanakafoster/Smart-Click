FAQ

Google Ads questions, answered

Can a business in Zimbabwe advertise on Google Ads?

Yes. Zimbabwe is a Google Ads billing country and isn't on Google's list of restricted countries, so a business based here can run Search, Display, YouTube, Performance Max, Demand Gen and Shopping campaigns aimed at Zimbabwe or at markets abroad. Google still reviews every ad against its policies, so approval isn't automatic.

How much should we budget for Google Ads?

It depends on how many people search for what you sell, how many competitors bid on the same searches and what a new customer is worth to you. We suggest a test budget after keyword research and adjust it once real results come in. Your ad spend is paid directly to Google, separately from our management fee.

Will our ads appear in Google's AI Overviews or AI Mode?

Possibly, in some countries. There's no separate purchase: ads in AI Overviews come from Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, and you can't target or opt out of that placement. Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode are only offered in the countries Google has opened them to, so we check your target market before launch. Ordinary search ads can still show above or below an AI Overview.

Can Google Ads reach Zimbabweans living abroad?

Yes. We target countries such as South Africa, the UK, the US and Australia, then focus on what diaspora buyers search for, like stands in Harare, school fees or groceries for family at home. We don't target people by nationality or ethnicity. We reach Zimbabweans abroad through location, interests, language and your own customer lists.
