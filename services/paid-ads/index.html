FAQ

Paid ads questions, answered

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, Performance Max and more), YouTube, Microsoft Advertising, Meta (Facebook, Instagram and Messenger), Click-to-WhatsApp ads, WhatsApp Status ads where Meta offers them, LinkedIn and programmatic display. TikTok and ChatGPT ads are available for diaspora campaigns run by businesses registered in countries where those platforms sell ads. Pinterest, Snapchat, X, Reddit, Spotify, Apple and Amazon ads are available on request, where the platform accepts your business and target market. The main platforms each have their own page with the details.

What should our ad budget be?

That depends on your goals, your industry and the value of each new customer to your business. We usually start with a test budget, learn what works, then scale up. Your ad spend is paid directly to the platforms.

Can you run ChatGPT ads for us?

Not for audiences in Zimbabwe yet. As of November 2026, OpenAI doesn't sell ChatGPT ads to Zimbabwe-registered businesses or show them to people in Zimbabwe. If your business is registered in a country where ChatGPT ads are sold and you want to reach customers there, we can plan and manage the campaigns. If not, we can prepare your creative, landing pages and tracking so you're ready if OpenAI opens to more countries, and our AEO service helps you appear in ChatGPT's answers in the meantime.

Can you reach Zimbabweans living abroad?

Yes. We can run campaigns for audiences in South Africa, the UK, the US, Australia and elsewhere, for things like property, education, groceries and family services. We reach them through location, interests and your own customer lists, never by targeting nationality.
