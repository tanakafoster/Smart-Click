Facebook and Instagram ads that turn scrolling into orders and enquiries

Our Facebook & Instagram ads service

Campaigns built in Ads Manager

We pick Meta's Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads or Sales objective to suit what you need, then set budgets, audiences and placements deliberately instead of leaning on the Boost button.

Placements and creative

Feeds, Stories and Reels on Facebook and Instagram, plus Messenger, each with creative made for it: vertical video for Reels and Stories, carousels and clear prices for feeds, in English, Shona or Ndebele. Threads ads run as an added placement on Facebook and Instagram campaigns.

Audiences and retargeting

Location, age and interests to find new customers, Custom Audiences built from your website visitors and from customer lists you have permission to use, and Lookalike Audiences that reach people similar to your buyers.

Lead forms and chat ads

Forms that open inside Facebook and Instagram, pre-filled with details people have already shared with Meta, or ads that open a Messenger or WhatsApp chat. New leads can flow straight into your CRM.

Catalogue ads for online stores

We connect your product feed so Meta can show items from your whole catalogue, with current images, descriptions and prices, to the people most likely to want them. They rely on the Meta Pixel, or on app events if you sell through an app.

Meta Pixel, Conversions API and GA4

The Meta Pixel and Conversions API record form fills and purchases on your site, GA4 key events capture WhatsApp and call clicks, and call tracking picks up phone enquiries. We can also send lead outcomes from your CRM back to Meta, so it learns which leads became customers.

Our approach

How we deliver Facebook & Instagram ads

Check accounts and tracking

We make sure your Facebook Page, Instagram account and ad account belong to your business, then install or repair the Meta Pixel, Conversions API and GA4 events.

Plan the offer and audience

We agree what you're selling, to whom and where, choose the objective and write ads that show the price and the next step.

Launch and test

Several versions of each ad go live side by side, so the results show which image, video, headline and audience earn their keep.

Scale and refresh

Every week we move budget towards the ads bringing in buyers, retire tired creative and check lead quality with your team, judging it all on cost per lead and cost per sale.

FAQ

Facebook & Instagram ads questions, answered

Can a business in Zimbabwe advertise on Facebook and Instagram?

Yes. Businesses based in Zimbabwe can run Meta ads aimed at customers at home and abroad. Which payment methods you can use depends on your country and currency, so we check them with you when we set up or review your ad account.

Isn't boosting a post enough?

Not quite. Boosting is a quick way to show an existing post to more people. A campaign built in Ads Manager lets us choose the objective, audiences and placements, run several ads against each other and track what each one brings in. Boosted posts can play a small part, but rarely the whole plan.

Do we need a website to run Facebook and Instagram ads?

No. Lead forms and ads that open a Messenger or WhatsApp chat work without one, which suits many small businesses, though lead forms must include a link to your privacy policy. A fast landing page still helps: it gives you room to explain your offer, builds trust and makes sales easier to track.

Can you reach Zimbabweans living in South Africa, the UK or elsewhere?

Yes. We target the countries and cities where your buyers live, use interests, language and your own customer lists, and write ads that speak clearly to people supporting family in Zimbabwe. We don't target anyone by nationality or ethnicity. If you advertise property, jobs or financial products, Meta's special ad category rules can limit targeting by age, gender, location and lookalike audiences in some countries, including the US, so we plan those campaigns around them.
